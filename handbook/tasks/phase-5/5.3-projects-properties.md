## Goal

The pages people come for. A visitor can:
- browse projects with filters kept in the URL;
- open a project and see everything the `NoirProject` board shows, with the RERA block, QR code and up-rera.in always visible;
- open a single configuration (a "property");
- browse all properties;
- land on a city's projects;
- see the project on a map.

All of this is server-rendered from the 5.1 API, honest by construction, and works without JavaScript for reading.

## Scope

1. **`/projects`:**
   - **Filters:** status, city, locality, type, BHK, budget, sort and text search, kept as URL parameters. A plain `<form method="get">` works without JavaScript.
   - **Results:** cards with cover, honest label, name, locality and city, configuration labels, the price range or "Price on request", the RERA number and the sold-out badge.
   - **Paging and states:** paging and an empty state.
   - **Order:** available projects come before sold-out ones, as the API already returns them.
2. **`/projects/<slug>`:**
   - **Header:** hero with honest label; name, tagline and status.
   - **RERA block:** authority, number(s) per phase or tower, the QR image and an up-rera.in link. It's always visible near the top and also in the footer of the page.
   - **Price:** price range with the note "excludes GST, stamp duty and registration". `price_on_request` hides every number.
   - **Configurations:** a table or cards, carpet area first in sq ft and sq m, each linking to its property page.
   - **Project content:** gallery and videos with honest labels; floor plans; amenities; specifications; location advantages.
   - **Construction progress:** dated updates with photos.
   - **Payment plans:** with their notes in `FormattedText`.
   - **FAQs:** native `<details>` elements.
   - **Brochure:**
     - not gated: a direct download;
     - gated: a "Get the brochure" link to the contact page, until 5.4 adds the form.
   - **Calls to action:** "Book a site visit" and "Enquire" go to the contact page for now (5.4 replaces them with forms).
3. **Property page:**
   - **Content:** one configuration with its own area, price, floor plan and status, plus its project's RERA block and price note.
   - **Path:** decide the path (`/projects/<slug>/<config-slug>` is the expected one) and record it in D-041 and `seo/paths.py`.
4. **`/properties`:** configurations across projects, with the contract's filters and sort.
5. **City pages:** a city's published projects. Decide the path, record it in D-041, add it to the reserved slugs if needed and add it to the sitemap feed.
6. **Map:** Leaflet with OpenStreetMap tiles.
   - **Loading:** lazy-loaded below the fold, behind a static "Show map" fallback that has the address and a Google Maps "Directions" link.
   - **Pop-up:** name, address, RERA number.
   - **CSP and attribution:** add the tile host to `img-src`, and show the OpenStreetMap attribution.
   - **Dependency:** pin `leaflet` after checking npm.
7. **Shared pieces** (used again in 5.5a):
   - `ProjectCard`, `PropertyCard`, `ReraBlock`, `PriceRange`, `AreaRange`;
   - the formatting helpers (₹ L/Cr, sq ft/sq m).

**Out of scope:** lead forms (5.4); metadata beyond `<title>` and structured data (5.6); EMI calculator and saved properties (5.7b); the blog (5.5b).

## Tests required

- **Unit:** money and area formatting (ranges, equal values, `price_on_request`), the filter-to-URL builder, and the RERA block (always rendered, multiple numbers).
- **API** (only if paths or the sitemap change): path helpers, sitemap entries, reserved slugs.
- **Browser:**
  - projects list filters by URL and form (with JavaScript off too);
  - the project page shows the RERA number, QR and up-rera.in link, and carpet area first;
  - `price_on_request` shows no numbers;
  - an off-market project returns 404;
  - property and city pages;
  - the map's fallback, then the map after clicking (tile requests stubbed in tests);
  - axe, CSP enforced with no violations, 390 px;
  - a publish in the CMS updates the project page after the drain, with a negative control.
