## Goal

Two small visitor tools from the boards, built so they can't mislead:
- an **EMI calculator** on property pages, with sample rates the visitor changes and a clear "indicative only, not a loan offer" note;
- **saved homes**: a "Save" button on property and project pages and a `/saved` page that lists them. These are kept only in the visitor's browser, with no account and nothing sent to us.

## Scope

1. **EMI calculator** (`components/tools/EmiCalculator.tsx`, client component; maths in `lib/tools/emi.ts`):
   - **Inputs:**
     - loan amount: whole rupees with Indian grouping (`NumberField`), prefilled from the home's lowest price when it is shown, and empty when the price is on request;
     - tenure: 5 to 30 years;
     - interest rate: 6.00 to 15.00 % a year, in steps of 0.05, defaulting to 8.50 %.
   - **Output:**
     - the monthly EMI, total interest, total paid and number of months;
     - the standard reducing-balance formula `EMI = P·r·(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1)`, with `r` the monthly rate;
     - a 0 % rate gives `P/n`;
     - shown in ₹ with Indian grouping, rounded to whole rupees.
   - **Honesty:**
     - the rate field says "sample rate, check with your bank";
     - under the result: "Indicative only, not a loan offer. Your bank decides the amount, rate and tenure.";
     - no bank names, no "lowest EMI" claims.
   - **Placement:** on the property page only, below the floor plan. It is not shown when the price is on request and the visitor hasn't typed an amount: it starts empty with a hint.
   - **Without JavaScript:** the block shows the formula and the note instead.
2. **Saved homes** (`components/tools/SaveButton.tsx`, `app/(site)/saved/page.tsx`, `lib/tools/saved.ts`):
   - **Save button** on the property page and the project page. It is a real `<button>` with `aria-pressed`, and its label changes between "Save" and "Saved". The change is announced politely.
   - **Storage:**
     - kept in `localStorage` under one key, as a list of `{ kind: "project" | "property", path, name, savedAt }`;
     - at most 50 items, newest first;
     - every read and write is wrapped in try/catch: storage can be blocked, and then the button says "Saving isn't available in this browser".
   - **`/saved` page:**
     - it lists the saved items, each a link to its page, with "Remove";
     - before showing an item it checks that the page still exists, through the public API on the site's own `/api` proxy (`GET /api/v1/public/projects/{slug}` or `/configurations/{config}`);
     - a 404 shows "No longer listed" with Remove, and never stale prices;
     - it doesn't show prices at all, only names and links, so nothing out of date is shown as fact;
     - an empty state with a link to Projects;
     - `noindex`.
   - **Privacy:** nothing is sent to us or anyone else when saving, and there are no cookies. Add one line to the page: "Saved only in this browser."
3. **No other change.** These two components are placed in the two page files with one line each, and nothing else in those files is touched.

**Out of scope:** share buttons (done in 5.5b); comparison; accounts; loan offers or bank partners; analytics events (Phase 6).
