## Goal

Every public page says correctly what it is and where it lives, to search engines and to link previews (WhatsApp, Facebook), and old addresses keep working.

## Scope

1. **Metadata** (`lib/public/seo.ts` `pageMetadata`) on `/`, `/<slug>`, `/projects`, `/projects/<slug>`, `/projects/<slug>/<config>`, `/properties`, `/cities/<slug>` and `/contact`:
   - `<title>` from the CMS's SEO title, else the page's own; the site's suffix comes from the SEO defaults in Settings (the home page's title stands alone).
   - Description: the CMS's SEO description, else the page's first text, as plain text up to 160 characters. Placeholders in `[brackets]` are never used.
   - Canonical URL on `SITE_URL` (production `https://empirebuildcon.com`). The CMS's canonical path wins when it is a site path. A filtered list's canonical is the list itself.
   - Open Graph and Twitter card: the share image is the CMS's share image, else the cover or first image (cropped to 1200×630 by Cloudinary), else the logo on black (`public/brand/share-default.png`).
   - `noindex` for landing pages and pages with `seo.noindex`. The whole site stays `noindex` until go-live (Phase 9 gate).
2. **Structured data** (JSON-LD, `components/JsonLd.tsx`, with the CSP nonce):
   - `Organization` on the home page.
   - `RealEstateListing` on project and home pages, with the UP-RERA number as its identifier, the address and pin, and the price only when the page shows one. The price is marked as excluding GST, stamp duty and registration.
   - A home's floor size is given only when its area is a carpet area; a range stays a range.
   - `FAQPage` on a project with FAQs; `BreadcrumbList` wherever the breadcrumbs show.
   - Nothing for sample data (no listing, FAQ or breadcrumb data). No ratings or reviews (testimonials are not reviews).
3. **Redirects** (`lib/public/redirects.ts`, `proxy.ts`):
   - A CMS redirect answers GET and HEAD with its 301 or 308.
     - The visitor's query string is kept (without Next.js's `_rsc`) unless the target has one, and goes before the target's `#fragment`.
     - `/`, `/admin`, `/api` and the site's own files are never redirected.
     - The answer may be kept by browsers for an hour.
   - The active list is kept for 60 seconds per server process (`REDIRECT_CACHE_SECONDS`).
     - An old list is used at once while a new one loads, so no page waits; only a process's first list is waited for, and for at most 800 ms.
     - If the API is down, the last list is kept and pages are served as usual.
   - Link prefetches and HEAD requests are not counted.
   - Each redirect served adds one to its hits (shown in the CMS), through `POST /api/v1/internal/redirects/hit`. That call is signed with `REVALIDATE_SECRET` over `redirect-hit.{ts}.{body}`. Headers are checked first and bodies over 1 KB are refused before they are read. The answer is the same 204 for any address.

**Out of scope:**
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` (5.6b, Track B).
- The blog's metadata: it comes from 5.5b, which can use `pageMetadata`.
- Hindi alternates (Phase 8).
- Go-live indexing (Phase 9).

## Tests required

- **Unit** (`test/seo.test.ts`, `test/redirects.test.ts`):
  - canonical, CMS fields first, no other host;
  - plain-text descriptions;
  - share image crop and default;
  - JSON-LD: escaping, RERA identifier, no price on request, carpet-area-only floor size, nothing for samples, no placeholders;
  - redirect matching, normalising, safe targets, never-redirected paths, one API call per minute, API down, hit signature.
- **API** (`tests/test_redirect_hits.py`): a signed hit counts; unsigned, wrong, stale and revalidation-style signatures are refused; unknown and inactive addresses change nothing.
- **Browser** (`qa/specs/seo.spec.ts`):
  - a project's head (title, canonical, description, `og:url`, `og:image`, Twitter card, `noindex` before go-live);
  - JSON-LD that parses, carries the nonce and has the RERA identifier and breadcrumbs;
  - no CSP violations;
  - a link-preview crawler gets the tags in `<head>`;
  - price on request leaves out `offers`;
  - a filtered list's canonical;
  - a page's CMS SEO fields;
  - the home page's Organization;
  - a redirect answered (308, query kept), counted, and a 404 once switched off.
