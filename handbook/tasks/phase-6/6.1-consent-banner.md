## Goal

When Empire starts running ads, visitors are asked once which cookies they allow, in a clear banner, before any tag loads. Their choice is kept in their browser and can be changed from the footer.

This task builds the banner and the stored choice only. Task 6.3 (Claude) then loads the tags according to that choice.

## Scope

1. **The stored choice** (`apps/web/src/lib/consent.ts`, new):
   - **Shape:** `type Consent = { v: 1; analytics: boolean; marketing: boolean; at: string }`. `at` is an ISO date. "Necessary" is always on and is not stored.
   - **Storage:** kept in `localStorage` under `empire.consent`. Every read and write is wrapped in `try`/`catch`.
     - A missing, unreadable or corrupt value, or a different `v`, means "not chosen yet": `readConsent()` returns `null`.
     - Storage that throws (a private window, blocked storage) still lets the visitor choose for the current page. Keep the choice in memory and say nothing about it.
   - **Expiry:** a choice older than 12 months counts as not chosen, so the banner asks again.
   - **Functions:**
     - `readConsent(): Consent | null`;
     - `writeConsent(choice: { analytics: boolean; marketing: boolean }): Consent`;
     - `subscribe(listener: (c: Consent | null) => void): () => void`;
     - `openConsentSettings(): void`, which re-opens the banner on its settings view.
   - **Events:** changes are announced in the same tab with a `CustomEvent("empire:consent")` on `window`, and across tabs through the `storage` event.

   Task 6.3 relies on exactly these names. Don't rename them.
2. **The banner** (`apps/web/src/components/consent/ConsentBanner.tsx`, client component, new):
   - **When it shows:**
     - only when the prop `enabled` is `true` and `readConsent()` is `null`, or after `openConsentSettings()`;
     - it renders nothing on the server and nothing before hydration, so there is no layout shift and no flash.
   - **First view:**
     - one short paragraph and a link to `/privacy`;
     - three buttons: **Accept all**, **Reject all** and **Choose**. Accept and Reject look the same and are the same size: same variant, side by side. Choose may be the quiet style.
   - **Choose view:** three rows.
     - **Necessary:** always on, shown as on and disabled, with one line on what it is.
     - **Analytics:** a switch, off by default.
     - **Marketing:** a switch, off by default.
     - Then **Save choices**.
     - When it opens from the footer, the switches show the stored choice.
   - **Layout:**
     - fixed to the bottom of the screen, full width on phones, a card at the bottom left on desktop;
     - on phones it sits above the `MobileActionBar` and never hides its buttons;
     - it doesn't block the page: no overlay, no focus trap, and the page scrolls behind it.
   - **Accessibility:**
     - a landmark: `role="region"` with `aria-label="Cookie choices"`;
     - a heading;
     - every control at least 44×44 px;
     - visible focus;
     - when it opens from the footer, focus moves to its heading;
     - after Save, Accept or Reject, it closes and focus goes back to the "Cookie settings" button if that opened it;
     - axe shows no serious or critical violation at 390 px and on desktop.
   - **Wording:** in one exported constant (`CONSENT_COPY`), every string starting with `[DRAFT]` until the legal reviewer approves it. A first draft:
     - **Heading:** "Cookies on this site".
     - **Text:** "We use necessary cookies to run this site. With your permission we'd also use analytics cookies to see how the site is used, and marketing cookies to measure our ads. Nothing is turned on until you choose."
     - **Rows:** "Necessary: needed for the site to work, always on." "Analytics: count visits and pages, without your name." "Marketing: measure which ads bring visitors."
3. **Mounting and the footer link:**
   - In `(site)/layout.tsx`, render `<ConsentBanner enabled={trackingEnabled} />` once.
     - `trackingEnabled` is `Boolean(site.tracking && (site.tracking.gtm_id || site.tracking.ga4_id || site.tracking.meta_pixel_id))`.
     - Today the API always sends `tracking: null`, so the banner stays hidden until 6.3. That is correct.
   - In `Footer.tsx`, add a **Cookie settings** button next to the legal links, shown only when `trackingEnabled`. It calls `openConsentSettings()`, and has the same style and 44 px target as the links.
     - Pass `trackingEnabled` down as a prop. Don't call the API again.

**Out of scope:**
- loading any tag or script;
- Google Consent Mode;
- CSP changes;
- the API sending tracking IDs;
- the privacy policy text.

All of these are 6.3 (Claude).

## Tests required

- **Vitest, `lib/consent.ts`:**
  - no value, a corrupt value or a wrong `v` gives `null`;
  - a value older than 12 months gives `null`;
  - write then read works;
  - storage that throws on read, on write and on the `localStorage` getter itself doesn't crash, and the choice holds in memory for the page;
  - `subscribe` fires on a write and on a `storage` event, and its unsubscribe works.
- **Vitest, `ConsentBanner` (jsdom):**
  - hidden when `enabled` is false;
  - hidden when a valid choice is stored;
  - shown when there is none;
  - Accept all stores `true`/`true`, Reject all stores `false`/`false`;
  - Choose shows both switches off, and Save stores exactly what is switched;
  - `openConsentSettings()` re-opens it on the Choose view with the stored values, and focus is on its heading;
  - the Accept and Reject buttons have the same classes.
- **Playwright** (`qa/specs/consent.spec.ts`):
  - **With today's API:** on `/`, `/projects` and a project page there is no "Cookie choices" region and no "Cookie settings" button. This is the launch state, and the test guards it.
  - **On a test page** (`qa/fixtures` or an `e2e`-only route, if you can do it without touching files outside your list): mount the banner with `enabled`, check axe at 390 px and on desktop, check that the mobile bar's buttons stay clickable, and check that the choice survives a reload.
  - If a test page isn't possible within your file list, say so in the report. The jsdom tests then carry those checks, and 6.3 adds the full e2e.
- **Failure proof:** for each behaviour, show the test failing without the code, then passing.
